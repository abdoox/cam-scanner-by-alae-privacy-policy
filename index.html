Privacy Policy

CamScanner (Android, package com.idc.camscanner) · Effective date: October 21, 2026

In short: CamScanner has no accounts and no cloud. Your scans, the text recognized in them and your settings are stored only on your device. We, the developer, do not receive your documents. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, which collects some device data as described below.

1. Who we are

This policy describes how the CamScanner app (“the app”, “we”) handles information. The developer is [DEVELOPER NAME]. Contact: abdelilahmouhibb@gmail.com.

2. Information that stays on your device

  • Scanned pages and imported images are saved as JPEG files in the app's private storage. Imported photos are re-encoded, which removes metadata such as GPS location (EXIF).
  • Recognized text (OCR) is produced on your device by Google ML Kit and stored in the app's local database so that search can find words inside your scans.
  • Document details (names, folders, favorites, dates) and settings are stored locally.

We do not upload, collect or have access to any of this information. Documents are excluded from Android cloud backup; only app settings may be included in your device's backup if you have it enabled.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: scanning uses the Google Play services document scanner, which handles the camera itself. The app does not record video or take photos in the background.
  • Storage: the app requests no storage permission. Files are written to shared storage (Downloads or Pictures) only when you choose “Save PDF” or “Save images to gallery”.
  • Biometrics: the optional app lock uses Android's system prompt. Your fingerprint or face data never reaches the app.
  • Internet: used to load ads, to obtain ad consent, and to download ML Kit models through Google Play services.

4. Sharing your documents

Documents leave the app only when you explicitly share, print or save them. When you share, the file is passed to the app you choose (for example email or messaging), and that app's privacy policy applies.

6. Your choices

  • Ad consent: where required, you are asked for consent before ads are personalized. You can change it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your Android settings (Privacy → Ads).
  • Deleting data: delete individual documents, use Settings → Delete all documents, or uninstall the app. Because we hold no copy, deletion on your device is final.

7. Security

Documents are kept in storage that other apps cannot read. You can add an app lock (fingerprint, face or PIN) and turn on Hide content to block screenshots and blur the app in the recent-apps screen.

8. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows the latest version; significant changes will be noted in the app's release notes.
